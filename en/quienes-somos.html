Who we are

Solid energy from plant waste

GEA Carbón is the production unit of GEA dedicated to making vegetable charcoal briquettes from recycled coconut shell, which we sell under the ECOCOCO brand, with a standardized transformation and pressing process.

We were born with a clear vision: to show that sustainability can be truly integrated into industry, with process control, defined technical criteria and continuous improvement.

We are part of the GEA Project (Grupo Empresarial Armas), a vision of sustainable development with an industrial, environmental and territorial focus, from Guatemala.

As a company that seeks a positive environmental impact, we prioritize making use of the coconut shell, an agro-industrial waste from the South Coast that would otherwise pile up or be burned. Learn the technical detail and our environmental commitment

Local production

We manufacture in Retalhuleu, with equipment designed and built here.

Circular economy

Every waste from the process is used as an input for another step.

Making use of coconut shell

We give a use to a regional agro-industrial waste instead of letting it pile up or be burned in the open air.

Local development

Industry as a tool to create economic value and sustainable growth in our region.

Mission and vision

We transform resources. We develop industry.

Mission

Turning resources into opportunities.

To develop and market energy solutions and products derived from the use of residual biomass, through innovation, applied technology and efficient production processes, turning agro-industrial resources into value-added products.

We work to drive the circular economy, strengthen Guatemalan industry, create job opportunities and contribute to the economic development of our communities, building productive capacity with responsibility, quality and business vision.

Vision

Building the industry of the future from Guatemala.

To become a leading Guatemalan company in the industrial transformation of biomass and the development of sustainable energy solutions, recognized for our capacity for innovation, engineering, our own technology and production excellence.

We aspire to build a diversified, technology-driven and competitive industrial platform, able to turn natural resources and agro-industrial waste into high-value-added products, expanding our operations into new markets and helping position Guatemala as a country capable of developing its own technology and industry.

Five strategic pillars

1

Circular economy

Turn agro-industrial waste into raw materials and economic opportunities.

2

Innovation and engineering

Design, build and refine our own machinery and technological processes.

3

Industrial development

Build productive capacity through mechanization, technical upgrading and continuous improvement.

4

Economic and social development

Create jobs, strengthen local suppliers and open opportunities for communities.

5

Expansion and competitiveness

Develop products, markets and capabilities to compete nationally and internationally.

Our philosophy"We do not see waste as refuse, but as resources with the potential to be transformed. We believe in our ability to develop technology, build industry and create opportunities from the resources available to us. Our philosophy rests on innovation, work, reinvestment, efficiency and sustainable growth."

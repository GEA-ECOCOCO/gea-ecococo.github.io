Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

What is the minimum order?

In Retalhuleu and the South Coast, 20 kg. For other departments, 50 kg. You can reach the minimum with 1 kg bags or by buying full 25 kg sacks.

What sizes do you sell?

A 1 kg bag (Q20) for households and shops, and a 25 kg sack (Q350) for wholesalers, with a special price by volume.

How do I place an order?

By WhatsApp (+502 4279-4894), by email (geaecococo.ventas@gmail.com) or with this site's cart, which builds your order and sends it on WhatsApp. An order is confirmed once we confirm quantity, price and delivery date.

What are the payment methods?

We accept bank transfer and deposit (Nexa and Banco Industrial) and cash on delivery, arranged when the order is placed. Sales are paid upfront. For shipments to other departments, payment is by transfer before dispatch. We issue FEL electronic invoices. We do not charge online for now.

Where do you publish the bank details?

We share them only by WhatsApp or email, with customers who have already confirmed their order. We do not publish account numbers on this site. If anyone asks you to pay into an account we did not confirm through those channels, do not pay and let us know.

How long does delivery take?

We are starting up production, so we confirm the delivery date when we receive each order. We arrange the day and delivery point with you.

Where do you deliver?

In Retalhuleu, with our own delivery. On the South Coast, to be arranged according to the order and route. To other departments, by carrier, with advance payment and freight paid by the customer.

Do you issue invoices?

Sales are documented in accordance with Guatemalan tax law. If you need an invoice, give us your name and NIT (tax ID) when you place the order.

What do I do if the product arrives damaged or incomplete?

Let us know on WhatsApp or by email within 48 hours of delivery, with photos and the name you ordered under.

Do you accept returns?

We do not accept returns of opened or used product, except for a proven defect. Returns of unopened product are reviewed case by case and agreed in writing before it is sent back.

What is ECOCOCO made of?

Made from coconut shell from the South Coast, carbonized and turned into a briquette. Our own formulation, developed by integrating raw materials of natural origin and in-house transformation processes.

How do I store and use the briquettes?

Store them in a dry, closed place, because moisture affects lighting. Use them only outdoors or in very well-ventilated places, never inside a house, tent or vehicle, because they produce carbon monoxide.

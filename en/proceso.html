How we make it

From shell to briquette

A simple process, made in Retalhuleu with regional raw material and recycled energy.

  1. STEP 01

    Coconut shell

    We collect shells, a local agricultural by-product.

  2. STEP 02

    Carbonization

    It is turned into charcoal in pyrolysis kilns.

  3. STEP 03

    Grinding

    The charcoal is ground to a fine powder.

  4. STEP 04

    Mixing

    It is combined with GEA's own formulation. Always with a mask and protective goggles.

  5. STEP 05

    Pressing and drying

    The briquette is formed and dried, ready for packing.

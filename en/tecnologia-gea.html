GEA Technology

Engineering, development and applied technology

We design and build in Retalhuleu the equipment we use to turn coconut shell into briquettes.

"We don't just develop products; we also develop the technology and processes needed to make them."

Where we are today

Small-scale mechanized production

We are getting a small-scale line up and running, with equipment made here and a clear vision for growth. We don't show a factory that doesn't exist yet: we show the real path, piece of equipment by piece of equipment, in the Plant log.

The nine stages

From shell to briquette

  1. STEP 01

    Receiving and sorting

    We receive the coconut shell and set aside what is unusable.

  2. STEP 02

    Shell preparation

    It is conditioned to enter the kiln.

  3. STEP 03

    Carbonization

    The shell is turned into charcoal in locally built pyrolysis kilns.

  4. STEP 04

    Crushing

    The charcoal is ground to a fine powder.

  5. STEP 05

    Preparing the mix

    It is prepared according to GEA's own formulation.

  6. STEP 06

    Blending

    The ground charcoal is combined with the prepared mixture. Always with a mask and protective goggles.

  7. STEP 07

    Pressing

    The briquette is given its shape.

  8. STEP 08

    Drying

    The briquettes are dried before packing.

  9. STEP 09

    Packing

    Packed in a 1 kg bag or a 25 kg sack.

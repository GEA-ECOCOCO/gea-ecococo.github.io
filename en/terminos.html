Terms and conditions

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · In case of any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who we are

This site belongs to GEA Carbón | ECOCOCO, a line of GEA Grupo Empresarial Armas, based in Retalhuleu, Guatemala. By placing an order or using this site you accept these terms.

2. Products

We sell coconut shell charcoal briquettes under the ECOCOCO brand. Sizes: 1 kg bag and 25 kg sack, the latter exclusive to wholesalers. The photos and videos on the site are for reference.

3. Prices

  • Prices are in quetzales (GTQ).
  • End consumer: Q20 per kg. 25 kg sack for wholesalers: Q350, with a special price by volume. Shops, distributors and restaurants: volume pricing, which we quote by WhatsApp or email.
  • Prices may change without notice. The price we confirm when accepting your order applies.
  • The calculator on the site gives an estimated total and is not an invoice or a commitment to sell.

4. Orders

  • Orders are placed by WhatsApp or email.
  • An order is confirmed once we confirm quantity, price and delivery date.
  • Minimum order for shops and distributors: 20 kg in Retalhuleu and the South Coast, and 50 kg for other departments. The minimum can be reached with 1 kg bags or with full 25 kg sacks.
  • The 25 kg sack is sold to wholesalers only. For other departments the minimum is 2 sacks (50 kg).

5. Payment and invoicing

  • Sales are cash. For shipments to other departments, payment is made by bank transfer before dispatch.
  • Bank details for transfers are shared only through our WhatsApp (+502 4279-4894) or our email (geaecococo.ventas@gmail.com), with customers who have already confirmed their order. We do not publish account numbers on this site. If anyone asks you to pay into an account we did not confirm through those channels, do not pay and let us know.
  • We accept bank transfer and deposit at Nexa and Banco Industrial, and cash on delivery, arranged when the order is placed.
  • We issue FEL electronic invoices in accordance with Guatemalan tax law. If you need an invoice, give us your name and NIT (tax ID) when placing the order.

6. Availability

Our production line is starting up. We confirm the delivery date when we receive each order, and it may vary depending on production.

7. Safe use of the product

  • Use the charcoal only outdoors or in very well-ventilated places. Never inside a house, tent or vehicle.
  • When burned it produces carbon monoxide, an odorless gas that can be deadly.
  • Let it cool completely before discarding the ashes, and keep the product away from children and pets.
  • Store the briquettes in a dry place.

Correct use of the product is the responsibility of the person using it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · In case of any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What data we receive

This site does not ask you to register and does not use tracking cookies by default. The data we receive is what you give us when you message us by WhatsApp or email, or when you fill in the distributor form: name, business name, phone, department, municipality, business address, type of business, commercial interest, estimated volume and comments. Your order cart is stored only on your own device; we do not receive it until you send it by WhatsApp.

2. What we use it for

  • Answer your questions and quote requests.
  • Arrange payment and delivery of your order.
  • Handle claims.
  • Review and respond to your distributor application.

We do not sell your data. We share it with third parties only when necessary to deliver your order, for example a transport company.

3. Third-party services

  • When you tap the WhatsApp, email or share buttons, you leave for those services, which have their own privacy policies.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data about the visit, such as the IP address.
  • The distributor form is sent to a GEA server on Cloudflare and recorded in our customer register, a private Google spreadsheet. Our sales team sees it there to contact you. If you share your number, we may send you an automatic WhatsApp message confirming your request. We ask only for the data we need and mark required fields with *.
  • Site assistant: if the chat uses artificial intelligence, the messages you type in it are sent to a third-party artificial intelligence service (Anthropic) to generate the reply. We do not store those conversations on this site, and we ask you not to type sensitive personal data. Answers are indicative: final prices and terms are confirmed via WhatsApp.
  • Assistant voice: if you use the chat microphone, voice recognition is done by your browser, which may send the audio to its own provider (for example, Google in Chrome). GEA does not receive or store the audio, only the resulting text. Reading answers aloud is done on your device.
  • Alerts (notifications): they are optional and only turn on if you ask for them and accept your browser's permission. To send them we only store the technical subscription identifier generated by your browser and the language of the page; we do not store your name or your location. We send at most two alerts per week with ECOCOCO news and offers. You can turn them off at any time from the page footer or from your browser settings.
  • If we enable anonymous analytics (Google Analytics), it only loads after you accept it in the page notice, and you may decline it.

4. Your rights

You can ask us to correct or delete the data you gave us by writing to geaecococo.ventas@gmail.com or by WhatsApp at +502 4279-4894.

5. Minors

This site is not directed at minors.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is the one published here, with its update date.

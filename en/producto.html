Our product

Meet ECOCOCO

Solid fuel made from recycled coconut shell. An agricultural by-product turned into useful energy for the home, the shop and the restaurant.

ECOCOCO

Our line for the home and the neighborhood shop, in a 1 kg bag.

What are our briquettes?

Vegetable charcoal briquettes made with a controlled process in Retalhuleu. Their compact, uniform shape supports stable combustion, better yield and more efficient use.

Ignition in 5 to 10 minutes

Approximate ignition time of 5 to 10 minutes, based on tests carried out by GEA. This is not the same as how long the embers last.

What does it mean?

Ignition time is how long the briquette takes to be ready for cooking. Combustion duration is a different parameter and is still pending validation; we will publish it when we have results.

Compact, uniform shape

A uniform briquette, made with controlled drying and compaction.

Why?

It is made with our own industrial drying and compression method that compacts the material into a dense piece. Strength results will be published once validated.

Regular, stable shape

Uniform pieces that sit evenly on the grill, stack better and allow even airflow.

Why?

We produce with machinery that gives every briquette the same size and a regular shape. Identical pieces sit evenly on the grill, stack better in the bag and let air pass evenly between them.

Steady heat

Ideal for grills, barbecues and kitchens that need sustained, predictable temperature.

Why?

When all briquettes have the same size and density, they burn at a similar rate and the heat of the embers stays even, with no zones that go out first and no pieces left unlit. That makes it easier to control cooking.

Performance and durability

We look for the balance between density and airflow to get steadier embers that last longer.

Responsible use

Made from agro-industrial waste, not from trees.

And how is it made with environmental care?

We make use of coconut shell, an agro-industrial waste from the South Coast, instead of using wood as raw material. Our plant's own measurements will be published in the Logbook once they are carried out.

Safety when using charcoal

  • Use it only outdoors or in very well-ventilated places. Never inside a house, tent or vehicle.
  • When burned it produces carbon monoxide, an odorless gas that can be deadly.
  • Let the ashes cool completely before discarding them. Keep children and pets away.

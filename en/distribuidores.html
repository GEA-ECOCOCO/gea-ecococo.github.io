Why ECOCOCO

Reasons for your business

Clear price per pound

The 25 kg sack costs Q350, equivalent to Q6.35 per pound. For larger volumes, we quote a special price.

Uniform product

A briquette of the same size and shape, easier to store, count and portion than irregular pieces.

An origin you can tell

Made from coconut shell, an agro-industrial waste of the South Coast, not from trees. A story your customers understand.

For restaurants and grill houses

Recurring orders with coordinated delivery, FEL electronic invoice and payment by transfer, deposit or cash on delivery.

Commercial terms

How we work

Volume pricing

We quote according to the quantity you need. Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply with the price.

Invoicing and support

We issue an FEL invoice with your name and NIT. We advise you on lighting and safe use of the charcoal so your customer has a good experience.

Packaging

1 kg kraft paper bag for display and sale. 25 kg sack for wholesalers.

For your business

A product with a story you can tell

If you have a shop, a restaurant or a grill, you offer a charcoal made from local coconut waste, without using firewood as raw material. It is one more reason for your customers to prefer it.

  • Local origin: coconut shell from the South Coast, a waste that would otherwise pile up or be burned.
  • No firewood: a charcoal alternative that does not require cutting down trees.
  • Ignition time: approximate, 5 to 10 minutes, based on tests carried out by GEA.
  • For your customers: you can tell them where what they buy comes from.

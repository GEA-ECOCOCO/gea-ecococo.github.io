Premium charcoal and coconut shell charcoal briquettes for grilling in Guatemala

ECOCOCO is a premium coconut shell charcoal in briquette form, made in Retalhuleu by GEA Carbón. It is an eco-friendly charcoal for grilling, barbecues and cooking, at home as well as in restaurants and stores. It is sold in 1 kg bags and 25 kg sacks for wholesalers, with delivery in Retalhuleu and the South Coast and shipping to other departments.

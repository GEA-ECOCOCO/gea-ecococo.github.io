Delivery and returns

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · In case of any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Delivery coverage

  • Retalhuleu: our own delivery, arranged by us.
  • South Coast: delivery to be arranged according to the order and route.
  • Other departments: shipping by carrier, with advance payment by transfer and freight paid by the customer. The 50 kg minimum order applies.

2. Delivery dates

We are starting up production. That is why we confirm the delivery date when we receive each order. We arrange the day and delivery point with you.

3. When you receive your order

  • Check that the bags or sacks arrive sealed, complete and undamaged.
  • For shipments by carrier, note any damage at the time of receipt.

4. Claims

If the product arrives damaged, incomplete or different from what you ordered, let us know on WhatsApp (+502 4279-4894) or by email (geaecococo.ventas@gmail.com) within 48 hours of delivery. Include photos and the name you ordered under. We review each case and, where appropriate, replace the product or adjust the order.

5. Returns

  • Because it is a consumer product, we do not accept returns of opened or used product, unless there is a proven defect.
  • Returns of unopened product are reviewed case by case and agreed in writing before it is sent back.

6. Storage

Moisture affects lighting. Store the charcoal in a dry, closed place.

7. Contact

WhatsApp +502 4279-4894 · geaecococo.ventas@gmail.com · Retalhuleu, Guatemala.

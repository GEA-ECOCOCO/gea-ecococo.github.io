GEA Consultora

Digital services for your business

Platforms that serve, register and follow up with your customers. It is the same technology ECOCOCO uses.

What your business gets

A platform that works with you

It is not just a page that shows information: it captures, organizes and helps you sell. These are its functions.

Serves customers when you are away

An artificial intelligence assistant answers questions about price, quantity and delivery at any hour and sends the customer to WhatsApp with the order ready.

Every contact is recorded

Forms arrive on their own in a spreadsheet with a status and a follow-up date. You know who still needs a reply.

Sells in fewer steps

The customer sees prices, builds an order and downloads a PDF proforma without waiting for someone to quote.

Installs as an app

Your business sits on the customer's phone, with no app store. With alerts you can share offers and news.

Easy to find and measure

Ready for search engines and Google ads, with visit tracking so you can decide where to invest.

Builds trust and reaches further

Privacy notice, spam protection, clear delivery and invoice information, and a Spanish and English version.

How we work

Three steps

  1. STEP 01

    We talk about your business

    We learn what you sell, to whom, and how people reach you today.

  2. STEP 02

    We build your platform

    We choose the functions you really need, no more and no less.

  3. STEP 03

    We publish it and stay with you

    We leave it running, under your own accounts, and teach you how to use it.
